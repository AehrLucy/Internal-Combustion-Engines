Problem 6: Otto Cycle with Polytropic Compression and Expansion

(Reference: Heywood, Ch. 5; Pulkrabek, Ch. 3)

A 4-cylinder, 4-stroke, 2.2 L gasoline engine operates on an Otto cycle with a compression ratio of 10. The air is at 100 kPa and 60 °C at the beginning of compression, and the maximum cycle pressure is 8 MPa. Compression and expansion may be modeled as polytropic with \(n=1.3\). Use constant specific heats evaluated at 850 K.

Calculate:

  • The temperature at the end of the expansion process.
  • The net work output per kg of air and per cylinder, in kJ.
  • The thermal efficiency.
  • The mean effective pressure, in kPa.
  • The engine speed, in rpm, for a net power output of 70 kW.
  • The specific fuel consumption, in g/kW·h, for an air-fuel ratio of 15.

Problem 7: Diesel Cycle with Combustion Efficiency

(Reference: Heywood, Ch. 5; Pulkrabek, Ch. 3)

A 6-cylinder, 4-stroke, 4.5 L diesel engine runs at 2000 rpm. At the start of compression the air is at 95 kPa and 55 °C and the compression ratio is 17. Compression and expansion are polytropic with \(n=1.3\), and heat is added at constant pressure. The fuel has a heating value of 42,500 kJ/kg, the combustion efficiency is 98%, and the mass air-fuel ratio is 24. Evaluate \(c_p\) and \(c_v\) at 950 K. Define the combustion efficiency as heat released to the gas divided by the fuel energy, \(\eta_c=m_a c_p (T_3-T_2)/(m_F\,HV)\), and the thermal efficiency as \(w_{net}/q_{in}\) with \(q_{in}=c_p(T_3-T_2)\).

Calculate:

  • The mass of air and the mass of fuel per cylinder per cycle.
  • The temperatures \(T_2\), \(T_3\) and \(T_4\) and the cutoff ratio.
  • The net work per kg of air, and per cylinder per cycle.
  • The thermal efficiency.
  • The mean effective pressure, in kPa.
  • The net power output, in kW, and the specific fuel consumption, in kg/kW·h.

Problem 8: Cylinder Cutout Test: Indicated Power and Mechanical Efficiency

(Reference: Pulkrabek, Ch. 2; Maleev, Internal Combustion Engines: Theory and Design (power and efficiency chapters))

A 4-cylinder inline engine has a bore of 75 mm, a stroke of 90 mm, 4 valves per cylinder and a compression ratio of 9.4:1. At 3500 rpm with one cylinder disabled (3 cylinders firing) the brake torque is 115 N·m. With all 4 cylinders firing at the same speed the brake torque is 160 N·m. Assume friction power is the same in both tests.

Calculate:

  • The total piston displacement, in cm\(^3\).
  • The brake power with 3 and with 4 cylinders firing.
  • Whether friction power changes between the two tests, with your reasoning.
  • The indicated power with 3 and with 4 cylinders firing.
  • The mechanical efficiency with 3 and with 4 cylinders firing.
  • The brake and indicated mean effective pressures, in kPa, for 3 and for 4 cylinders firing.

Problem 9: Diesel Generator Set Operating at High Elevation

(Reference: Pulkrabek, Ch. 2; Class notes (no textbook cited; instructor to confirm a reference))

An 8-cylinder, 4-stroke, single-acting diesel engine rated at 933 kW generator output at standard conditions is directly coupled to a 24-pole, 3-phase, 60 Hz alternator. Brake mean effective pressure is 517 kPa and average piston speed is 305 m/min. The combined fuel consumption is 0.161 kg/kW·h (based on generator output), the lower heating value is 46,526 kJ/kg, mechanical efficiency is 85%, generator efficiency is 94% and relative humidity is 90%. Standard sea-level conditions are 101.325 kPa and 15.56 °C. The engine is later operated in Baguio City, 1525 m above sea level, where the sea-level temperature is 29 °C (take \(P_{sat}\) at 29 °C = 4.008 kPa and assume friction power is the same at all conditions).
Given correction relations :

\[B.P._0=B.P._s\left(\frac{P_s}{P_0-P_v}\right)\sqrt{\frac{T_0}{T_s}},\qquad B.P._h=B.P._0\left[\,r-\frac{1-r}{7.55}\,\right],\qquad r=\frac{\rho_h}{\rho_0}\]

\[P_h=P_0-11.107\,\frac{h}{1000}\ \text{kPa},\qquad t_h=t_0-6.5\,\frac{h}{1000}\ ^\circ C,\qquad \rho=\frac{P}{RT}\]

Calculate:

  • The alternator speed and the stroke and bore of the engine, in mm.
  • The actual brake thermal efficiency at standard conditions, in %.
  • The brake power at Baguio City, in kW.
  • The mechanical efficiency at Baguio City, in %.
