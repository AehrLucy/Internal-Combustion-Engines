Practice problems

Check Your Understanding

  1. Find \((A/F)_s\) and the LHV of C₃H₈ given HHV = 50,350 kJ/kg and hydrogen mass fraction 0.1827.
  2. A diesel engine burns C₁₂H₂₃ at \(\phi=0.6\). Write the balanced equation per kmol of fuel and find the dry O₂ percentage.
  3. An ethanol (C₂H₅OH) engine runs at \(\lambda=1.1\). Compute \((A/F)_s\), the actual \(A/F\) and \(\phi\).
  4. A biomass sample has C 0.48, H 0.06, O 0.40, ash 0.02, moisture 0.04 (mass fractions). Compute HHV by the Dulong formula and the theoretical air.
  5. Extend Example 9 with brake power 45 kW and exhaust at 650 °C. Draw the heat balance table and Sankey diagram.
  6. Show how raising excess air from 10% to 40% changes the dry flue gas loss of a boiler. State your assumptions.
  7. A CI engine in Unit I Problem Set 1 (Problem 4) needs 1400 kJ/kg of air as heat input. With LHV = 42,500 kJ/kg, \((A/F)_s=14.5\) and \(\eta_c=0.95\), find the equivalence ratio.

Self-study checklist

  • ☐ I can write and balance combustion equations with air for any C-H-O fuel.
  • ☐ I can convert HHV to LHV and choose the right one.
  • ☐ I can compute \(\phi\), \(\lambda\) and \(A/F\) from engine flow data.
  • ☐ I can convert between wet and dry product analysis.
  • ☐ I can complete a heat and mass balance for an engine and a boiler.
